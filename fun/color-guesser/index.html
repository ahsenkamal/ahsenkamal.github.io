Color Guesser

How well do you know your colors?

A random color is shown below. Guess its RGB values (or hex). After each guess you get a hint for every channel: ↑ go higher, ↓ go lower, ✓ exact.

    Find the target color. Click or drag on the wheel to pick hue and saturation, and use the lightness slider. Submit when you think you have it.
